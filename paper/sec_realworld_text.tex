Model~D is trained on the first $70\%$ of a $260$\,k-byte WikiText-2 stream ($182$\,k bytes), ranked and fine-tuned on the next $15\%$, and scored once on the final $15\%$ ($39$\,k bytes) at the long horizon $L=512$: it reaches $2.882$ bits per byte against a unigram baseline of $4.579$, i.e.\ 37.1\% of the unigram entropy removed, with one byte per step and an event timestep that is doubled at sentence boundaries.

Model~E consumes ten real CIF clips ($4\,410$ frames at $16\times16$ grey levels, $150$--$300$ frames each, 25--30\,fps) and predicts the next frame's $256$ pixel values, with the same $70/15/15$ split applied inside every clip ($1\,874$ / $417$ / $419$ frames): held-out $R^2=0.900$ against $0.0054$ MSE for the zero predictor ($0.2787$), with $\Delta t=1/30$\,s fixed by the camera and the frame-to-frame noise, illumination drift and scene cuts of real footage present at every step. The clips are real camera footage (surveillance, traffic, news, hand-held nature); no synthetic stream enters Model~E.

At $\rho=1/4$ the decoupled forward with the Jacobian ordering costs $+0.526$ BPD on text and $-0.023$ $R^2$ on frames, against $+0.806$ BPD and $-0.648$ $R^2$ when the hot set is chosen by index order, so the ordering matters on real streams exactly as it does on the synthetic ones.

Closed-form readout recalibration on decoupled states (fp64, one Cholesky solve, $12$--$56$\,ms per solve at these sizes) does exactly what it is supposed to do and no more. It \emph{rescues} the approximation where the head has no chance: the pure-linear decoupled model degenerates to $-2.708$ $R^2$ on frames and $3.980$ BPD on text, and one linear solve on states it has never seen lifts the frame model to $0.831$ $R^2$, i.e.\ most of the exact model's quality. At $\rho=1/4$, where the trained head is already near-optimal, the same refit moves frames from $0.877$ to $0.851$ $R^2$ (a small loss) and text from $3.408$ to $7.643$ BPD (a large one: least squares on one-hot targets is not the maximum-likelihood head, so for a 256-way softmax readout the closed form is the wrong estimator). We report both directions: the cheap recalibration of Section~\ref{sec:express} buys accuracy in approximation regimes and cannot improve on a head trained end-to-end.

Five epochs of DAFT on the hot block only move the text model from $3.564\to3.054$ BPD at $\rho=1/8$ and from $3.408\to2.923$ BPD at $\rho=1/4$, and the frame model from $0.734\to0.876$ $R^2$ at $\rho=1/8$ and $0.877\to0.877$ at $\rho=1/4$, so the real streams behave like Parity-Stream and FlipFlop-Reset rather than like the oscillator: the decoupled fit recovers with a short fine-tune, not with a new architecture. No configuration in either table beats the exact model on the held-out region, which is the property an approximation guarantee should have and which a leaky split would destroy.

End-to-end, the same Triton path that serves the synthetic sweep gives $6.8\times$/$3.9\times$ over the strongest sequential baseline at $\rho=1/4$ on these two real streams, with the single-launch sequential kernel used as the baseline wherever it beats the PyTorch loop.
